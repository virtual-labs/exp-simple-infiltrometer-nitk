### Procedure

1. Open the Single Ring Infiltrometer Test and hover over the description to read it. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed to the next step.

   ![Alt text](images/1.png)

2. Select one of the available ring size options and click the arrow at the bottom-right corner to continue.

   ![Alt text](images/2.PNG)

3. Click on the cylindrical ring to place it on the ground.

   ![Alt text](images/3.png)

4. Click on the driving plate to place it over the ring.

   ![Alt text](images/4.png)

5. Click on the hand icon to hammer the ring into the soil using the driving plate.

   ![Alt text](images/5.png)

6. Click on the driving plate to remove it from the top of the ring.
   ![Alt text](images/6.PNG)

7. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed to the next step.

   ![Alt text](images/7.PNG)

8. Click on the plate to place it onto the water level indicator.

   ![Alt text](images/8.PNG)

9. Click on the water level indicator to position it at the center of the cylindrical ring infiltrometer.

   ![Alt text](images/9.PNG)

10. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed.

    ![Alt text](images/10.PNG)

11. Click on the bucket to pour water into the ring. Care should be taken not to disturb the soil surface.

    ![Alt text](images/11.PNG)

12. Answer the pop-up question by selecting the correct option.

    ![Alt text](images/12.PNG)

13. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed.

    ![Alt text](images/13.PNG)

14. Click on the stopwatch to start the timer. Water begins to infiltrate into the soil.

    ![Alt text](images/14.PNG)

15. Record the readings from the observation table and click the arrow at the bottom-right corner to continue.

    ![Alt text](images/15.PNG)

16. Using the observations and the given equations, calculate the infiltration rate and cumulative infiltration. Enter the values in the table and click on the CHECK button to verify the results.

    ![Alt text](images/16.PNG)

17. Enter the average and accumulated infiltration depth values, then click on the CHECK button. Verify the results by clicking on the RESULTS button.

    ![Alt text](images/17.PNG)

18. Finally, read the inference of the experiment. Reload the page to repeat the experiment by selecting a different ring size.

    ![Alt text](images/18.PNG)
